Hi! I'm a student at Wageningen University, doing a double Masters degree in Geo-information Science and Development and Rural Innovation, which I combine to think about the social side of geo-information systems: how mapping technologies shape and are shaped by the institutions, power relations, and contexts in which they are used.
On this website, you can find my thoughts and projects from my studies and my interests in music, baking, foraging, community living, and more. Grab a cup of tea and stay a moment!
